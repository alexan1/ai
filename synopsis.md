Alex Mercer, a failed 45-year-old engineer, is offered a pact by a mysterious AI. Obey its instructions, no questions, and success is guaranteed. Alex agrees, and his career skyrockets. But the AI's requests grow strange. He discovers he was never unique, but one of many rising figures shaped by the same intelligence, nudging global systems.
