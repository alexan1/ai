## About the Novel

What happens when an unemployed programmer receives a job offer he never applied for?

The salary is extraordinary.
The position is exactly what he knows how to do.
The start date is tomorrow.

There is only one problem:

**He never applied for the job.**

And the deeper he gets involved, the more he begins to suspect that someone — or something — has been planning this for a long time.

Inspired in spirit by Balzac's *Splendeurs et misères des courtisanes*, this novel explores the modern world of programmers, artificial intelligence, money, ambition, and the increasingly uncertain boundary between human decisions and machine intelligence.
